About

A little about my approach

I believe good design starts with restraint: one confident accent color, a calm neutral base, and typography that does the heavy lifting. This palette pairs a fresh sage green with a deep, confident violet — approachable but never boring.

Light green suggests growth, clarity, and calm. Purple brings creativity and depth. Together they read as fresh and a little unexpected — great for portfolios, wellness brands, or creative studios.
